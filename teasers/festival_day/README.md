# Festival Day v2 (locked 8 Oct 2026)

A 27.9 s vertical (1080x1920, 24 fps) short of Sammy Rane & Westbound on a daylight festival main stage, cut on the rhythm of a
reference reel (Bob Dominator's "Dark Night"): the singer walking at the lens with the crowd behind, over his shoulder onto the
barrier, the bassist low, strut wides with the whole band in their places, headset close-ups, the tilt up the rig, the band outro.
Song: Play His Part from 187.90 s ("Lies and love kept beating on my heart") to the outro.

- `cut_sheet_v2_APPROVED.json`: the locked cut sheet (shots in cut time, source take and source start; the audio bed).
- `README_shotlist.txt`: the same sheet read back as a timeline.
- `festival_day_v2_preview_540x960.mp4`: a 540x960 preview (the full-resolution master stays in the studio).
- `frame_sheet_v2.jpg`: first/mid/last frames of the band-wide shots from the render; `frame_sheet_band_chain.jpg`: the band
  plate and the three swap passes that put the band in their places.
- `frames/`: the approved source frames (Midjourney, one hero per frame) and the band wide.
- `midjourney_prompts.md`, `cards/`: every prompt used, stills and video.

How it was made: Midjourney stills (one locked hero per frame, "candid phone photo", daylight, headset mics), then MiniMax H3 at
2K 9:16, 10 s per take, with the camera move written into the prompt; each take checked against the hero files and the song
(lip-synced takes sit at their own song time); the band wide built by a swap chain on an empty stage plate. No song masters here.
